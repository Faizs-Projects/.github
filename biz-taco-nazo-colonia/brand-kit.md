# Brand kit — Taco Nazo, Colonia

Public-source notes for the demo. Nothing here is a printed brand guide from the shop. They do not have a first-party website.

## What this place is

Independent counter taqueria at 1331 St Georges Ave, Colonia, NJ 07067 (Woodbridge Township). Phone (732) 943-2006. A lot out front, a few counter seats, takeout and delivery. Pets are listed as welcome. Families, lunch breaks, students, and repeat takeout orders are who the public reviews describe.

It is not the California chain. `taconazo.com` lists La Puente, South El Monte, Bellflower, Walnut, La Habra, and Santa Ana only. That company (Taco Nazo Corp, Covina, CA) holds the live USPTO mark TACO NAZO, registration 7519114, serial 97935187, filed May 13, 2023, registered October 1, 2024, classes 39 and 43, first use claimed 1979. Their story is Ensenada-style fish tacos since 1978. The Colonia menu is a different restaurant: birria with consommé, huaraches, mole, breakfast from 9, weekend pozole. A John F. Kennedy Memorial High School article called the Colonia shop a Los Angeles franchise. That claim does not match the chain's own location list or the menu, and this site does not repeat it.

Using the name on a public site raises the visibility of a possible trademark conflict. The footer says the Colonia restaurant is independent and not affiliated with Taco Nazo Corp. Confirm with the owner before this demo is indexed or advertised.

## Voice and color

No logo files, no hex codes, and no site of their own. Apple Maps points the website field at Yelp: https://www.yelp.com/biz/taco-nazo-colonia

Instagram handle tied to this address in public captions: https://www.instagram.com/taconazonj/ — the profile itself did not load for this build. No TikTok account confirmed for the Colonia shop. Do not link the California chain's accounts.

The California chain's fish mark is blue and orange. This demo does not use it. The palette is taken from the food, not from that trademark:

| Role | Hex | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ground | `#12100E` | Comal, the night counter |
| Surface | `#F4EFE6` | Masa |
| Ink | `#1C1612` | Text |
| Accent | `#9D1C14` | Dried chile. Passes on masa. Fails on comal, so it is not used there |
| Highlight | `#E7F07A` | The order ticket and the phone number |

Type: Darker Grotesque for the poster and the menu names, Outfit for everything you read in a parking lot. Both are OFL and self-hosted.

## Menu

Dishes on the page are ones that show up on both DoorDash's category list and at least one full menu scrape (MenuToEat, checked against a second scrape), or that a dated public review names (birria, elote, camarones rancheros, torta magnum, tres leches, chicken birria). Prices are not printed. The two scrapes disagree, and the page sends people to DoorDash for the number the kitchen rings up.

## Photographs

No food photos are embedded. The pictures online sit on Google, Yelp, and Instagram, and this demo does not republish them. The hero is type. When the owner sends photos, they belong in the four plates, not in a stock-slider.

## Competitors, briefly

The St. Georges corridor and nearby towns are full of generic Mexican sites and chains (El Ranchito, Macho Nacho, Chipotle, Tacoria, Fat Cactus). The usual page opens on a red-and-gold slider and the words "authentic Mexican food," and makes you hunt for the phone. This one opens on the dish people already name, with DoorDash and the counter number on the same screen.
